# Citation Discipline

When making claims about papers:

- Use stable identifiers when available: Zotero key, DOI, arXiv id, BibTeX key, or local paper id.
- Distinguish direct claims from inferred relationships.
- Mark missing evidence with `needs_verification: true`.
- Do not invent citations or bibliographic metadata.
- If a claim depends on a source snippet, include a short pointer to the relevant section or page when available.

# Skill: Weekly Synthesis

## Purpose

Turn the week's reading and notes into project-level research memory by reconnecting old and new knowledge.

## Inputs

- Recently changed paper cards.
- Recently changed concept and method cards.
- Project context files.
- Open questions.
- Previous weekly synthesis notes.

## Output

A weekly synthesis note under `06_synthesis/weekly_synthesis/`.

## Workflow

1. Identify files changed or added this week.
2. Retrieve older notes connected by trigger terms, questions, methods, and project seeds.
3. Group updates by active question, not just by paper.
4. Summarize what changed in understanding.
5. List old notes that became relevant again.
6. Identify emerging themes, conflicts, and missing evidence.
7. Promote useful questions into `04_questions/open_questions.md`.
8. Update project context only when the direction or retrieval seeds changed.
9. Record 3-5 next actions for the following week.
10. Write a trace under `agent/traces/`.

## Rules

- Do not re-summarize every paper mechanically.
- Focus on changes in understanding.
- Separate strong conclusions from weak signals.
- Prefer 3-5 meaningful next actions over a long task list.
- Do not create broad new structure during weekly review.

## Quality Checklist

- It is clear what changed this week.
- Important papers are linked.
- Research questions are updated.
- Old notes that resurfaced are named.
- Next actions are concrete.
- Uncertainty is visible.

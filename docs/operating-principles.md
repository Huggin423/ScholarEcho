# Operating Principles

## Keep Zotero and the Vault Separate

Zotero remains the source of truth for bibliographic metadata and PDFs. The vault is the source of truth for structured research understanding.

## Prefer Small Structured Notes

Long summaries are easy to produce and hard to reuse. Prefer smaller, typed notes with stable sections and links.

## Require Traceability

Claims should point back to a paper, a user note, or a clear inference. If the evidence is weak, mark it.

## Make Projects the Center of Gravity

Every paper should eventually connect to a project, question, method, or concept. A paper with no connection is only stored, not integrated.

## Automate Slowly

Start with Markdown and manual review. Add MCP, vector search, and automatic writes after the information architecture feels stable.
